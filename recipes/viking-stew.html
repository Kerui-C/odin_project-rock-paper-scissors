Viking-Stew

A staple in the viking diet this rich Viking-Stew
is made with slow cooked beef or other meats and root
vegetables and spices

  • 1 lb or cubed beef or venison
  • 2 onions
  • 3 carrots
  • 1 tbsp salt?
  • 2 tbsp of butter
  • 4 cups of beef broth
  1. in a large pot, melt butter
    over med heat and cook the
    onions until soft

  2. add your meat until browned ayo?

  3. stir in your carrots salt
    and any additional vegetables

  4. bring to boil and lower heat
    and simmer for 1-2 hours
